Imagine that you are at a party and someone asks you where you work. You say at company X and tey pull out their phone and see the website that you build.

Points of view

- from an engineer: proud that what you built is running on their phone
- from a designer:
  - frustrated that what you are seeing is not what you came up with, at least not with a lot of asterisks and caveats.
  - reputation is tainted because they are judged ion what people can see in a real world product
  - the situation can lead to resentment and conflict

Collaborative reactions

- "nits": (Take the Concerns Seriously)

  - prioritize the easier problems
  - research and brainstorm that remaining without investing many hours
  - DM designer to get feedback on potential solutions

- Finding this on the design technically challenging

  - start a conversation to see if there are alternatives
  - see if you can de scope
  - maybe you can get rid of it altogether
  - always explain explain

- If you get done early, maybe DM people asking if there are other things they would like you to look at

- early involvement

  - important that devs are involved early
  - important that designers are involved through the building phase
  - send GIFs or pictures of local prototypes
  - if possible deploy to a blob storage, so that the designer can be hands on

- important to develop a design intuition

  - learning why the things are the way they are in a design system
  - speak with designers and learn why they structured things the way they did
