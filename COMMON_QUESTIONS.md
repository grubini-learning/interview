# Questions

1. Tell me about yourself?
   - share concrete products you worked on
   - use keyword buzzwords
   - answer why are you a good fit for this role
2. Why do you want to work at this company?
3. What is the biggest achievement?
4. How do you handle pressure?
5. Why this position?
6. What is the biggest strength/weakness?
7. What do you know about our company?
8. Tell me about a situation where
   - demonstrated leadership
   - took initiative
   - manage crisis
   - handled a difficult colleague
   - demonstrated teamwork skills
   - You made a mistake
9. What your previous boss would say about you?
10. What do you like the most / least about this industry
11. Where do you see yourself in 3 to 5 years.
12. Why was there a gap in employment?
13. What are your career goals?
14. What do you like to accomplish in the first X month in the job?
15. Why did you leave your last job?
16. Who are our biggest competitors and why are they doing well?
17. What motivates you?
18. Tell me about a time that you had to deal with a conflict
    - show that you have dealt with common challenges
    - focus on traits or actions that you tell in these stories
19. A time where you had to deal with a failure?
    - show vulnerability and awareness about my mistakes
    - what are your learnings
    - what are you going to do different with what you learned
    - looking for
      - humility
      - vulnerability
20. Tell me about a time that you committed and couldn't deliver?
    - show empathy, put yourself in their shoes
    - project and team management skills
21. Tell me about a time that you had to deliver a piece of tough feedback?
22. What are you looking for in your next role?

## Tips

- think deeply of 5 - 10 career stories
- write down work experiences so you don't forget

## Surprise question

- jot down the question
- jot down the traits that you want to convey

## Strategy

1. Write down the questions that you think you could be asked.
2. Answer these questions.
3. Revise the questions and modify to make them more powerful and compelling.
4. Come up with bullet points after for each question.
5. Grab postits and one side write the question and in the other the bullet points.
6. Put them in a hat and mix them up.
7. Grab one and answer the question.
   - if errors, check bullet points and put it back on the hat.

## Behavioral format

- tell a story
- 1 - 3 minutes long
- keep explanation easy to understand

## Story toolbox

      - notable career moments
      - PAR: Problem, Action, Result
      - Have the title of the stories next to the screen
