# Walkthrough

This application's goal was to assist in generating sales while providing an easier experience for the sales representatives to captivate and grow their client base as well as manage their data.

## Sign in 0.19 minutes

- The application supports admin and sales roles. This could be accessed through the three vertical dots on the right corner of the screen.

- They had different sales groups through the country. The app used the url to know which sales sub division the user belonged to

## Dashboard 0.33 minutes

This screen allows the user to have an overview of the client visits for the day, their quota, and their metrics by product category.

## Clients 1:15 - 4:02 minutes

This section has details about the client from the point of view of the sales representative.

- clients outside of your route for the day
  - can filter clients
  - order creation
  - indicate "no order" for this visit
  - contact information
- edit client information

## Orders 4:04 - 6:10 minutes

- product search
- promotions
- quick access to cancel order
- order details
- finalize order
- offline capabilities
  - side menu that contains the page where pending orders are located to send at a later time

## Cancellation 6:15 - 6:20 minutes

- cancellation questionnaire

## Improvements

This application's goal was to assist in generating sales while providing an easier experience for the reps to captivate and grow their client base as well as manage their data.

- filtering products by voice command
- having a map that would outline the route with the clients along the way that the sales rep had to visit
  - correct location an estimated time of arrivals
  - immediate status updates of what they have to do for the day
  - sense of progress
