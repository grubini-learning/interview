## Tell me about one of your goals and how did you make it happen?

When was young I had a brain aneurism, in which i lost all fine and thick motor skills. I had to re learn everything, even think like an adult again. My goal was to regain fine motor control and to be able to type at high rate again, since software development is my passion. I bought a split off keyboard and practiced for two hours everyday in typingclub.com. I am glad to say that I am no longer hindered by it and was successful at completing my goal.
