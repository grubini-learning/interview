# Questions

## San Fernando - Fire \*\*

During my time with San Fernando, I was a manager of a team of engineers. One of them was turning in work quite late which was causing a shift in project workflow and deliverables. Through talking to him, I realized he lived in a rural area and his electricity would randomly shut off. After learning this, I offered the solution of working together out of an area that had consistent wifi and electricity so he could complete his work in a timely manner. Initially, he agreed but did not follow through. After a few more chances, we identified that this was no longer a good fit and I had to deliver the news that we had to let him go due to performance issues. It was a difficult conversation that taught me how to have compassion while still upholding company standards. As a result, I had to take on his work load and was able to complete it by the deadline that was given to us by the product owners

### Highlights

- empathy
- leadership
- difficult situations
- goal oriented
- communicative

## DSB Mobile - Sexy Beast

One application I worked on was a Canadian app called Sexy Beast. The purpose was to facilitate dates based off of the love of pet owners and to create a romantic connection. However, it was developed originally in IoS. The clients wanted to transfer the application to React Native so that it could also be utilized on Android and IPhone. With this experience, there were a few challenges such as language barriers between the client and my fellow teammates and working in different countries. However, we were able to overcome these as I was put as the manager and lead of the project. Throughout this time, I noticed a flaw in the design of the sign up process that disrupted the user experience because there was no way to exit out of it. I brought this concern to one of the higher stakeholders and he agreed. We corrected the issue and found a higher satisfaction rate from the IOs to react Native.

## Microsoft - Sunny

There was a fellow engineer who was having issues completing tasks. During a meeting, he unfortunately became somewhat hostile and erratic towards the other engineers on the team. One in particular was getting the brunt of it and I stepped in to diffuse the situation. I calmly offered to pair program and assist him with his task. He agreed and we moved on to the next topic in the meeting. Before the pair programming, I identified from the ticket the acceptance criteria and looked at what he had to identify what needed to be done. Once I got into the meeting, I was faced with having to adapt my original approach due to him wanting to proceed in a specific way. We ended up creating a new workspace for him and transferring his work folder by folder. By the end, we came to the conclusion that it was simply environment configuration errors in his system and we were able to complete his task.

### Highlights

- leadership
- proactive
- level headed
- confident
- plan oriented
- collaborative
- team player
