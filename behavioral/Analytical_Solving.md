## Microsoft - Search Result Will/Nick/I/Design

There was a search result page that involved three engineers in my team, myself included. Will was tasked with implementing the search result page leveraging a "Search Result" and the "AutoSuggestions" component implemented by Nick and myself. They had asked me to pair program to sync the components, but this was proving to be difficult since there was a discrepancy in the data that each component required. I noticed we were all getting frustrated with each other. So, I decided to diffuse the situation by taking a step back to reiterate our goal. After this moment, we arrived to the conclusion that there were too many holes with the search result component and that it wasn’t ready for production. So, we decided to leverage the existing components within our library to solve the issue since the purpose of the Search Result component was purely presentational. Once getting an initial working prototype, we leveraged cross-functional communication with the design team to follow an iterative approach with an acceptance criteria to push the project into production. We were being celebrated through multitude of teams due to the fact that it made their jobs easier.
