## PFG - Cypress testing

At Principal Financial, we had team OKRs and had to increase the test coverage from 50% to 80%. To meet the acceptance criteria, a teammate and I started by looking for areas where we could test our code base and came to the conclusion that we didn’t have any end-to-end tests. We started diving into different options like SmartBear and Cypress, in the end we chose Cypress because of the easiness of use, documentation and community support. Over the course of our investigation, our initial findings for this framework transitioned into a guide for other engineers as it became a staple tool within the company.

### Highlights

- initiative
- resourcefulness
- research
- logical
- analytical
- teamwork

## Microsoft - receiving feedback in microsoft doc

In the process of implementing a new component/feature, after design finalized their specs, they were handed off to engineering and I had to begin the planning and estimation phase. When I felt good with my results I had to present to the whole team with an online Microsoft document that allowed for others to have context before the meeting, provide observations of possible resources, questions, and comments for unaccounted scenarios that could hinder the end result. I would make sure to leave a trace to show the history of the document which would help me at a later time in personal retrospectives. One of the pieces of feedback was for me to provide an easier development experience for our users and for them to have a seamless usage of the component. I was a bit uncertain of how to go about this and asked the engineer that made the comment for help. She pointed me to an area in the code base where we exhibited the behavior and I successfully implemented the experience. Through this process I encouraged feedback from not only my team, but from others as well.

### Highlights

- teamwork
- receives feedback well
- organized
- awareness
- humbleness

## Microsoft - onboarding documentation

In every company I have worked for, I always try to pass down my knowledge for those who are new. I feel for other engineers that have to go through this process as it is not easy. At Microsoft in particular, there were steps I had to go through that were not documented. Because of this, I decided to take the initiative and created diagram flows for new team members. It proved to be an effective method as I had multiple people reach out and mention that it helped them a great deal. This small detail fostered better relationships within the team, and improved their productivity because they were able to get to coding faster.

### Highlights

- team work
- proactive
- organized
- analytical
- considerate
