## Microsoft - Help Filtering

One of my fellow team members was blocked and reached out asking if I could assist him with a Filtering component. I told him yes and we hopped on a call. Because I find time boxing to be the most effective method of working for me, I communicated the block of time I could give. I knew he had done components like this before so I tried to make suggestions to nudge him in the correct direction. This way, he could learn and grow as an engineer as well as completing his task. When we reached the end of the allotted time, I once again communicated with him that I had to hop off. He understood and we got off the call. Once I completed my tasks, I circled back to ask if he needed any more assistance. He said he had actually figured it out and we were able to celebrate his success!

### Highlights

- leadership
- team work
- confidence
- disciplined
