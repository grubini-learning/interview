## San Fernando - Redux + SQLLite \*\*

Where the designer began spearheading a project for the sales force of a whole food seller. After being brought to the team in one of the scheduled meetings I decided to ask the product owner where the app was going to be used. They explained that it was going to be in areas where there was limited wifi. Because of this, I needed to come up with a complex solution for the orders to be saved when wifi was out of reach. I saw this as an opportunity to promote effective cross-team communication as I needed multiple deliverables in order to complete my tasks. From my end, I needed to be able to share information across the application as well as a database to promote redundancy and resiliency for the data. The design team needed to provide a visual that communicated this to the sales representative and the backend needed to create a way to receive a list of orders to be registered in one request. Everyone was effective within their areas and we were able to achieve our goal in a collaborative environment.

### Highlights

- collaboration
- goal oriented
- lift requirements
- improved user experience
- understands consumer behavior

## Microsoft - Web Components

**web component was uncertain**, A project was particularly ambiguous was when I was working on a team to create a component library using Web Components technologies. We were given a general idea of what the expectations were, but there were a lot of details that were left up to us to figure out. To approach this ambiguity, I started to schedule meetings with the purpose of writing a product brief that would depict different layers that provided not only a sense of progress, visualization and composition, but also allowed the team to provide immediate feedback, define the needs of the users, stakeholders and a clear definition of success to our finish line. Before and during the execution process I created meetings with the interested stakeholders to enable communication, specially with the borders around my team to make sure we were on track and that the needs of the end users were being met. Through effective communication, openness, problem-solving and clarity, we were able to complete the implementation of this library with its first twenty fully tested, accessible components and variations within a three tier approach.

### Highlights

- ambiguity
- team work
- goal oriented
- communication
